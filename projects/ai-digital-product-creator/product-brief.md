# Product Brief — SMART KIDS – ENGLISH ADVENTURES

**Status:** Planning / existing-material audit required  
**Product type:** Printable English-learning workbook (PDF)  
**Market:** International / Gumroad  
**Language:** English  
**Audience:** Children ages 6–10

## Product concept

A colourful, beginner-friendly English workbook that helps children practise vocabulary, spelling, basic grammar, reading, writing, and simple problem-solving through short activities and games.

## Learning levels

### Little Learners (ages 6–7)
- Short, familiar words and simple instructions.
- Letter recognition, basic spelling, matching, tracing, and sentence completion.
- Picture-led activities with limited writing.

### Young Explorers (ages 8–10)
- Wider vocabulary and slightly longer reading passages.
- Sentence building, basic grammar, spelling patterns, and short writing tasks.
- More independent puzzles, comprehension, and challenges.

The two levels must feel connected but must not simply repeat identical exercises with harder numbers.

## Format and design direction

- Printable PDF, designed for A4 first.
- International English; use clear, age-appropriate wording.
- Pastel accents, plenty of white space, and a printer-friendly white background.
- Friendly cartoon animal characters as visual guides.
- Large, readable headings and rounded, legible body fonts.
- Consistent page structure and clear activity instructions.
- Avoid crowded pages, tiny text, and excessive ink-heavy backgrounds.

## Quality requirements

- Every activity must have one clear learning objective.
- Instructions must be grammatically correct and understandable to the target age.
- Answer keys must match the activities exactly.
- Both levels must be clearly labelled.
- No copyrighted characters, branded artwork, or copied workbook pages.
- Check every page at normal screen size and in a printed-page preview.
- Ensure page numbering, margins, contrast, and answer spaces are consistent.
