**Levels:** Little Learners (6–7) and Young Explorers (8–10)

## Level differentiation rule

Where a page is shared by both levels, provide two clearly labelled task tracks:
- **Little Learners:** shorter words, visual clues, matching, tracing, and one-step instructions.
- **Young Explorers:** fuller sentences, broader vocabulary, short reading, and multi-step tasks.

Do not force the same worksheet onto both age groups. Review cognitive load, reading length, handwriting space, and vocabulary difficulty separately.

## Page design checklist

- One main activity per page (or a clearly grouped pair of short activities).
- Clear title and short instruction.
- Visible level label when tasks differ.
- Large answer spaces for younger children.
- Consistent footer/page number.
- Printer-friendly white background and restrained pastel accents.
